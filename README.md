# MINI-MART SALES DASHBOARD – Q1 2026

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Mini-Mart sales performance for the first quarter of 2026 (Q1 2026) using data analysis and interactive dashboard visualization.

The project focuses on understanding sales trends, product performance, salesperson performance, regional sales, customer types, payment methods, and sales ratings.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw Mini-Mart sales data into meaningful business insights that can help management:

* Monitor overall sales performance
* Identify top-performing products
* Evaluate salesperson performance
* Compare sales across regions
* Understand new versus returning customers
* Analyze payment methods
* Evaluate sales performance ratings
* Track monthly sales trends
* Support data-driven business decisions

## Dashboard Analysis

### Monthly Sales Trend

The dashboard analyzes sales performance across January, February, and March 2026.

* January: ₦21.3M
* February: ₦25.6M
* March: ₦18.4M

February recorded the highest monthly sales during Q1 2026.

### Salesperson Performance

The dashboard compares sales generated by different salespersons.

Top-performing salespersons included:

1. Femi Alabi — ₦15.9M
2. Ibrahim Musa — ₦13.6M
3. Adaeze Obi — ₦9.3M

This analysis helps management identify strong sales performers and evaluate individual contributions.

### Sales by Region

Sales performance was analyzed across:

* Abuja
* Ibadan
* Kano
* Lagos
* Port Harcourt

Abuja recorded approximately ₦21M in sales, followed by Ibadan at approximately ₦18M.

### Customer Analysis

The dashboard compares:

* New Customers
* Returning Customers

Returning customers accounted for approximately 58.3% while new customers accounted for approximately 41.6%.

### Payment Method Analysis

Customer payment methods were analyzed using:

* Cash
* Transfer
* Card

Cash represented the largest share of recorded payment methods at approximately 45%.

### Sales Rating Analysis

Orders were categorized using sales ratings:

* Low
* Medium
* High
* Excellent

The analysis helps identify the distribution of sales performance across different rating levels.

### Product Performance

Top-performing products included:

* Beans (50kg bag) — ₦19.5M
* Vegetable Oil (25L) — ₦9.5M
* Rice (50kg bag) — ₦8.5M
* Granulated Sugar (50kg) — ₦8.1M
* Wheat Flour (50kg) — ₦6.6M

Beans (50kg bag) generated the highest sales among the listed products.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

* February was the strongest sales month in Q1 2026.
* Beans (50kg bag) was the leading product by sales.
* Femi Alabi was the top-performing salesperson.
* Abuja recorded the highest regional sales.
* Returning customers represented a larger share than new customers.
* Cash was the most frequently recorded payment method.

## Business Recommendations

Based on these findings, management could:

1. Increase inventory for high-performing products.
2. Investigate why sales declined in March.
3. Reward and motivate high-performing salespersons.
4. Develop strategies to retain returning customers.
5. Improve marketing efforts to attract new customers.
6. Monitor regional performance to identify growth opportunities.
7. Encourage convenient digital payment options.
